## 🇬🇧 English

### Shooting is hard. Managing jobs is harder.

Are you a freelance photographer? Every day you juggle client messages, shoot schedules, delivery deadlines, deposits, remaining payments, travel costs and gear expenses — scattered across chats, notes and memory. Miss one thing and you lose a client, money, or your reputation.

**FotoBro** brings it all together in one place: from the moment a client asks for a quote, through the shoot, to delivering photos and collecting full payment. You focus on shooting beautifully — FotoBro handles the rest.

### Built the photographer's way, not the accountant's way

No complex software to learn. Open the app and instantly see today: which jobs are coming up, which clients haven't paid their deposit, which photos are due. Every task takes just a few taps and finishes in 10 seconds — because your time belongs behind the camera, not behind paperwork.

### Free to start, private by design

FotoBro v1 is completely free, and your data stays on your iPhone — no complicated accounts, no leaking client information. One-tap Google sign-in brings in your calendar and contacts; one more tap imports your iPhone Calendar. Vietnamese or English, VND or USD — your choice.

## ✨ 5 tính năng nổi bật | 5 standout features

1. **📋 Pipeline 8 bước — 8-step job pipeline**
   Tư vấn → Chốt cọc → Sắp chụp → Đã chụp → Chọn ảnh → Hậu kỳ → Đã giao → Xong. Nhảy thẳng tới bước bất kỳ, không lo sót việc.
   *Consult → Deposit → Scheduled → Shot → Selected → Editing → Delivered → Done. Jump to any step, never miss a thing.*

2. **💰 Sổ tiền & nhắc thu tiền — Money ledger & payment reminders**
   Ghi cọc ở bước Chốt cọc, nhắc thu nốt khi giao ảnh, cảnh báo nếu job xong mà còn thiếu tiền.
   *Record deposits, get reminded to collect the balance on delivery, and get warned if a finished job still has money outstanding.*

3. **🧮 Máy tính giá Digital / Film / Cả hai — Price calculator for Digital / Film / Both**
   Tính giá nhanh theo từng gói, giá film riêng từng gói do bạn tự đặt trong Cài đặt.
   *Quote fast by package, with separate film pricing per package that you set yourself in Settings.*

4. **🔐 Khoá Face ID / PIN & giọng nói — Face ID / PIN lock & voice control**
   Dữ liệu job và tiền bạc được khoá an toàn; ra lệnh bằng giọng nói tiếng Việt khi đang bận tay.
   *Your jobs and money stay locked and safe; control the app by Vietnamese voice commands when your hands are busy.*

5. **📅 Google 1 chạm & Lịch iPhone native — One-tap Google & native iPhone Calendar**
   Đăng nhập Google một chạm là có hết lịch và liên hệ; nhập Lịch iPhone trực tiếp, nhắc lịch ngay trong app.
   *One-tap Google sign-in brings your calendar and contacts; import iPhone Calendar directly, with reminders right in the app.*
